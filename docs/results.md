# Results
Generated by `midas research report` from `data\daily.pkl` (daily bars 2004-01-02 to 2026-11-06). Do not edit by hand; rerun the command.
## Method
- Adjusted daily closes from yfinance. Cash is the 13-week T-bill yield (^IRX); Sharpe is excess over that cash series, so holding T-bills scores 0.
- Costs: 5 bp per side on ETFs, 10 bp on single stocks, charged on turnover. Monthly strategies read the month-end close and trade the next open; daily filters trade only when the signal flips.
- Cross-sectional rows use today's S&P 500 list and therefore carry survivorship bias; the equal-weight row measures that bias against SPY.
- The Midas clone is the committee's deterministic rule set with the LLM's stock picking replaced by the scored screen. It measures the framing (tight stops, time stops, throttled exposure), not any model.

## Entry rules as event studies (S&P 500, today's list)
Each table asks whether a signal day is followed by a better return: the signal is read on a completed bar, the entry is the next open, the exit is the close `horizon` sessions later, 10 bp a side. `raw_net` is the mean return per trade after costs and `excess` the mean over SPY held across the same window, both in percent and both means of daily means; `t_excess` is Newey-West with `horizon` lags; `win_net` is the share of signals above zero after costs. Survivorship lifts every row, so a row is read against the baseline beside it, not against zero.
