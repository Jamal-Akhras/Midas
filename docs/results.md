## Method
- Adjusted daily closes from yfinance. Cash is the 13-week T-bill yield (^IRX); Sharpe is excess over that cash series, so holding T-bills scores 0.
- Costs: 5 bp per side on ETFs, 10 bp on single stocks, charged on turnover. Monthly strategies read the month-end close and trade the next open; daily filters trade only when the signal flips.
- Cross-sectional rows use today's S&P 500 list and therefore carry survivorship bias; the equal-weight row measures that bias against SPY.
- The Midas clone is the committee's deterministic rule set with the LLM's stock picking replaced by the scored screen. It measures the framing (tight stops, time stops, throttled exposure), not any model.
